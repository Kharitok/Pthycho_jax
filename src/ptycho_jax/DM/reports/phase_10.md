Object error removes scalar and affine phase on a fixed illuminated support (normal diagonal >5% maximum); the full-geometric-coverage error is also retained in JSON. Unaligned translation is not removed. Mode-specific correlations are reported only for matched modes (overlap >=0.8); unmatched/constant cases are null. F_fixed is also the negative control for the same varying-weight truth used in D. Thresholds are indicative, and a failed entry remains visible rather than changing a tolerance. The scalar/mode gauges can limit parameter recovery even when the measured-data fit is accurate.


A/B meet the R targets and object errors <1e-2 after using the specified known fixed amplitudes. C meets its R target but coherence overlap ≈0.88 misses 0.95. D meets its R target but not every matched-mode weight correlation exceeds 0.8. E remains above 1.1× the generating-parameter noise floor after 200 iterations. F per-shot weights improve both fit and object error versus frozen mean weights. A/B are already near noiseless truth; a fixed-rate 50-epoch AD trial slightly perturbs their approximately 1e-6 MSE rather than improving it. Translation alignment is deferred. Synthetic generator seeds are deterministic; GPU scatter-reduction order can cause small run-to-run floating-point changes in nonlinear DM.

All departures and numerical settings are documented in [decisions](../docs/decisions.md).
